Findings

Ranked by what it costs you

1. Two live domains for one store

P0 · Critical
Issue
Both sutherlinkia.com (new) and orangecountykia.com (legacy) resolve as live dealership sites. The legacy domain even renders the new title tag while its body still says “Car Pros Kia.” One store, two front doors.
Evidence
Google indexes both hosts for identical inventory; legacy interior pages (/promotions/index.htm, /service/center.htm) still carry Car Pros branding at the same address & phone.
Impact
High. Link authority, crawl budget, and ranking signals split across two domains — neither ranks as well as one consolidated site. Pixels & analytics fragment too.
Fix
Pick the canonical domain (recommend sutherlinkia.com). 301-redirect every legacy URL to its exact equivalent — page-to-page, not blanket-to-home. Keep the old domain redirecting 12+ months. Verify both in Search Console and file Change of Address.

2. Migrate the Google Business Profile in place — never recreate it

P0 · Critical
Issue
The classic rebrand mistake is spinning up a new profile for “Sutherlin Kia” and orphaning the decade-old Car Pros Kia listing with all its reviews and local ranking history.
Impact
Severe. A new listing starts at zero reviews and zero local authority; Google may flag a same-address duplicate and suppress both.
Fix
Edit the existing profile's name to “Sutherlin Kia Huntington Beach,” update the URL/photos/hours. Google preserves reviews and ranking through an in-place name change. Merge or remove any duplicate — never run both.

3. Name inconsistency across the citation graph

P0 · Critical
Issue
The store's name reads “Car Pros Kia” on Yelp, Cars.com, CARFAX, KBB, DealerRater & CarGurus — but “Sutherlin Kia” on the site. Address and phones are consistent (good); the name mismatch alone breaks Google's entity resolution.
Impact
High on map-pack ranking and on AI answer engines.
Fix
Citation cleanup across the top 20 directories — start with the Kia OEM locator, Google, Bing, Apple Maps, then the big-4 shopping sites. Do the OEM locator and maps by hand; a tool accelerates the long tail.

4. No public “we've rebranded” bridge

P1 · High
Issue
Nothing connects “Car Pros Kia” ↔ “Sutherlin Kia.” Returning customers Googling the old name can't tell it's the same store, same building, same service department.
Impact
High on returning-customer retention and service-drive traffic — the most profitable traffic a dealer has.
Fix
Persistent “Car Pros Kia is now Sutherlin Kia — same team, same location” notice on-site, in the Google profile description, and on a short FAQ/press page. Protects branded search for years.

5. Reputation: great asset, wrong label

P1 · High
Issue
1,700+ Yelp and 460+ Cars.com reviews all live under “Car Pros Kia.” Under the new brand, fresh shoppers see a near-empty history and assume you're unproven.
Impact
High on close rate — reviews are the #1 trust factor in auto retail.
Fix
Preserve legacy reviews via in-place renames; launch an aggressive review-generation cadence (SMS/email at every sale & RO); add a Google-review-backed testimonials strip on-site; respond to every review within 24–48h.

6. Technical SEO on a legacy templated build

P1 · High
Issue
URL fingerprint (/new-inventory/index.htm, /service/center.htm) is the classic Cox Dealer.com / DealerOn-family pattern — OEM-compliant but shared by thousands of stores. [verify vendor + package]
Checks
Duplicate content across two domains (canonicals likely not cross-pointing) · self-canonical hygiene on VDP/SRP · brand strings still mixed in templates · Core Web Vitals on mobile [verify] · Local/Vehicle/Review schema must carry the new name [verify] · legacy sitemap still feeding Google.
Impact
High (duplicate/canonical) → Medium (speed/schema).
Fix
After domain consolidation, run a rendered crawl + PageSpeed on home/SRP/VDP + Rich Results validation; ticket by severity.

7. Conversion & analytics hygiene

P2 · Verify
Tune
One primary CTA per template · trim lead-form fields to name + contact + vehicle · one-tap click-to-call above the fold + sticky mobile call/text bar · confirm chat/text route into the CRM (not a dead widget) · digital-retail tools (payment, trade, pre-qual) lead-connected · service scheduler prominent and on the surviving domain.
Critical
Rebuild GA4 + call tracking + form events on the canonical domain. A rebrand + domain split is the #1 cause of “our leads dropped” that's actually just broken tracking.

8. AI / Answer-Engine readiness (where we live)

P1 · High
Issue
Shoppers now ask ChatGPT, AI Overviews & Perplexity “best Kia dealer near Huntington Beach.” They answer from consistent, structured entity data — and right now they see a fractured entity: two domains, two names, reviews on a brand you dropped.
Impact
High and growing — the fastest-shifting acquisition channel in auto retail, and you're near-invisible or mislabeled in it.
Fix
Findings 1–3 are the AEO foundation. Then add complete AutoDealer / LocalBusiness + Review + FAQ schema with the new name, and publish a crisp factual “About Sutherlin Kia Huntington Beach” entity page (incl. the Car Pros → Sutherlin history) that engines can quote verbatim.
