**Strategic call (Michael, 2026-08-18):** win the LOCAL war for AIPG + WD by flanking, not fighting the
established national names. **Verified with data** and refined: the flank is real, and the winning
vehicle is **NOT microsites** (they split authority, start at zero, read as doorway/PBN, and add decay
surface). The whitespace is on the **one brand domain + third-party directories** — both of which
*build* the entity instead of fragmenting it.

## First domino (honest blocker) — escalate to Michael
The "look like a credible local entity" half depends on **Google Business Profile**, which is gated on
**entity**: AIPG has **no LLC**, and GBP needs a verifiable business + address + human verification.
Content cannot substitute. AIPG entity → GBP is the unlock for the whole AIPG local track.
See [[project_entity_hygiene]]. This is a Michael-hands item.

## AIPG track
1. **GBP + reviews** (gated on entity) — local answers cite local businesses + Yelp/Birdeye, so AIPG
   must read as a local service entity, not a SaaS page.
2. **Query framing fix** — target *"answering service for [trade] in [city]"* (open field). **Drop the
   "AI receptionist" framing on local pages** — the probe showed it summons Smith.ai even locally.
3. **Deepen the location pages we already have** (`/answering-service-{trade}-{city}`) with real local
   specifics (response times, neighborhoods, local trade detail) + FAQPage + LocalBusiness schema +
   un-orphan them from nav. Owner: IM writes / B&T ships / Iris gate.
4. **Open-field targets first:** plumbers-Frisco, electricians-Plano, then scale trade×small-city.

## WD track

   Clutch, Expertise.com, DesignRush, Bark, BBB, Semrush agency directory, and the hyper-local
