## Part B — Claim the Google Business Profiles (google.com/business)

### GBP rules that keep you un-suspended (read once)
- **Business name = the exact DBA name, nothing appended.** "The AI Phone Guy" — NOT "The AI Phone Guy
  Answering Service HVAC Plumbing." Keyword-stuffing the name is the #1 suspension trigger.
- **Service-area business:** during setup, choose "I deliver goods and services to my customers,"
  **hide the address**, and set the service-area cities.
- **Verification:** SABs usually get **video verification** — be ready to film the branded material,
  tools/workspace, and the business in operation. Have the DBA certificate handy.
- **No CMRA / PO box** as the address.
- **No fabricated reviews or claims** (same hard rule as the Zoe-fabrication gate — every claim real).
