## Three cross-cutting patterns (act on these first)
1. **G2 owns the "[competitor] alternatives" queries** — showed up for "Fullpath alternatives" and
   "AgencyBloc alternatives". A claimed, complete **G2 profile** is a fast citation lever for **Bookd**
   (B2B SaaS) and **AVI**.
2. **Reddit is the single most-repeated source** — cited on AVI BDC, Bookd (2×), and Agent Empire (3×).
   Authentic participation in the right subreddits is the highest-frequency third-party lever in the
   whole portfolio (draft-and-a-human-posts; never botted).
3. **Niche tool-list / directory sites answer "best X"** — dealershipaitools.com (AVI),
   insuraitools.com (Bookd). Getting listed on these is cheaper than out-ranking the incumbents.

## Strategic read for CMO
Comparison pages help, but the data says the **faster** Phase-3 lever is **getting listed where AI already
looks** — G2/Capterra/GetApp for the SaaS brands, niche tool-lists for AVI, and authentic Reddit across
all three. That's cheaper and faster than out-authoritying Creatio or Fullpath head-on, and it's the same
flanking logic that the AIPG/WD local probe confirmed. Sequence: claim the directory/review profiles first,
build the comparison pages second, out-rank the incumbents last.
