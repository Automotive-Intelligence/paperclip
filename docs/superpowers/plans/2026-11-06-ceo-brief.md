# CEO Brief: one daily email replaces every AVO system stream

Approved by Michael 2026-11-06 (handed off by Mikey). Builder: Team Principal.

## Intent

Michael gets about 10 system emails a day across three inboxes. Replace them with one
daily CEO Brief to michael@automotiveintelligence.io plus a dashboard page with the same
numbers. Immediate sends stay only for a real lead and a shoot-day failure.

## Streams and where they go

| Stream (sender) | Today | After |
|---|---|---|
| Morning Briefing (briefing@, 8:00) | daily, also to salesdroid | job removed; data folded into the Brief |
| AI spend (ape@, 7:55) | daily | job removed; "Money" section |
| APE autopilot digest (ape@, 18:00) | per persona | job removed; "Shipped" section |
| CMO Daily (cmo@, 7:00, to WD inbox) | daily | job removed; "Shipped" + "Broken signals" |
| Sonar "need a human" (cmo@) | per sweep | filed to brief_items; "Needs you" |
| Postal "emails need you" (briefing@) | per sweep + SMS | filed to brief_items; "Needs you"; no SMS |
| Script engine FAILED (briefing@) | weekly | filed; immediate only when the failure reason is new |
| Infrastructure CRITICAL (briefing@) | on red | filed; email only if INFRA_CRITICAL_EMAIL=on |
| Lead reconcile delta (cmo@) | on delta | filed; "Broken signals" |
| Client Portal per-event (portal@) | per event | "live" notices dropped; the rest gated by PORTAL_NOTIFY_MODE=brief; /api/brief feed |
| Community Desk LISTENING/REACH (leads@) | desk-1 VM | already silent: desk-1 and social-1 are TERMINATED |
| Real lead (leads@ / cmo@ lead_capture, Book'd) | immediate | unchanged |
| Funnel canary RED (cmo@) | immediate on transition | unchanged (a red canary = leads being lost) |

## Pieces

- `services/brief_items.py`: durable "needs you" ledger (Postgres `brief_items`), deduped by fingerprint, never raises.
- `services/ceo_brief.py`: collect -> render (email + page) -> send -> persist (`ceo_brief_runs`).
- `app.py`: `ceo_brief_daily` job (CEO_BRIEF_HOUR, default 7:00 CT), `GET /brief?k=`, `GET /brief.json?k=`, `POST /admin/ceo-brief-now`.
- client-portal: `GET /api/brief` (bearer PORTAL_BRIEF_KEY), notify gating.

## Order inside the Brief

1. Needs you today (empty = one line)
2. Money
3. Scoreboard (5 businesses, one-liners for the minors only when something moves)
4. Broken signals (one line per dead feed, repeated until fixed)
5. Shipped yesterday, per brand

Every section collapses to one line when empty. The Brief sends on zero days.
